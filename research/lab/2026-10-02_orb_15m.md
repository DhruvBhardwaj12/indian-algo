# LAB-020 — Opening Range Breakout (15m bars, NSE)

## Hypothesis
The first 15 minutes of the NSE session establish a range. A breakout
above (long) or below (short) with 2:1 R:R captures continuation.

## Rules (frozen)
- Opening range: high/low of first 15-min bar (9:15-9:30 IST)
- Entry: next bar breaks above high (long) or below low (short)
- Stop: opposite side of opening range
- Target: 2× risk
- EOD exit: at close if neither hit
- Max 1 trade per symbol per day
- Notional: ₹20,000 per trade

## Cost model
- Brokerage: ₹12 round trip (Zerodha discount tier)
- STT, exchange, GST, stamp: ₹9
- Slippage: 3 bps/side = ₹12
- Total: ~₹34 = 17 bps round trip

## Results (60 days, 9 symbols — TATAMOTORS data missing)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total trades | 518 |
| Win rate | 39.6% |
| Avg win | +₹130 |
| Avg loss | −₹124 |
| Profit factor | 0.70 |
| Net P&L | −₹12,544 |
| Per-symbol winners | 2/9 (ADANIENT +₹322, SBIN +₹44) |

## Exit breakdown
- Target hits: 60 (11.6%)
- Stop hits: 178 (34.4%)
- EOD exits: 280 (54.0%)

## Verdict: REJECTED

The strategy has a small positive gross edge (~+₹10/trade ≈ 5 bps)
but the round-trip cost of ~17 bps is 3.4x larger. Net per trade
is −₹24.

The high EOD exit rate (54%) is diagnostic: the 2:1 fixed target
is rarely hit within a session. Most breakouts fade back into range.

## Key observation
Same pattern as crypto: signal exists, cost overwhelms it. This
time the market is different but the mechanism is identical.

## What we haven't tested (future experiments)
1. Trailing stop instead of fixed target (may capture trending days)
2. Tighter stop (1:1 R:R, more stops, smaller losses)
3. Volume filter (fewer trades, lower cost drag)
4. Larger target (3:1 R:R, fewer hits, bigger wins)
5. Different timeframe (30m ORB)
6. Different strategy family (VWAP+EMA, 9/21 cross, range-bound)

Each will be tested as a separate frozen experiment.
