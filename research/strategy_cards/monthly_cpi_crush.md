Why This Works So Well
US CPI (Inflation data) is the single most important macro event in global markets. It is released at 6:00 PM IST, when the Indian market is closed.

On the day before (T-1), Indian option sellers demand massive premiums because they are terrified of waking up to a gap.

By the time the Indian market opens the next morning (T), the US market has already digested the CPI data. The fear is gone. The IV Crush is violent.

The Only Risk (The -₹6,315 Loss)
Look at the Sept 2024 trade. The Iron Condor lost -₹6,315. This happened because Nifty gapped down massively through our 500-point wing.
Because we bought the wing, we didn't lose ₹20,000 (which would have blown up the account). We lost the maximum width of the spread, which was capped at ₹6,315. That is a 31% drawdown. It hurts, but it is mathematically survivable.
